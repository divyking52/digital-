Process

How it works

A clear, compliant path from registration to settlement — designed for institutional recovery teams and qualified bidders.

Swipe for steps →

01

Register

Institutions onboard via dedicated portals. Bidders create verified profiles with organization credentials and contact details.

02

KYC Verification

Mandatory KYC checks for bidders — PAN, Aadhaar, and entity documents — ensuring only eligible participants enter the auction room.

03

EMD Payment

Earnest Money Deposit is collected digitally against the listed asset, with automated receipts and bank-reconciled ledgers.

04

Bid Online

Live bidding with real-time increments, auto-extensions, and full audit logging — accessible on desktop and mobile.

05

Win & Settlement

Successful bidders receive confirmation; institutions manage sale confirmation, balance payment, and asset handover workflows.
